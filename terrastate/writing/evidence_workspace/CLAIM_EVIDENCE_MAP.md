Status vocabulary: **supported**, **partially supported**, **unsupported**.
“Supported” is always conditional on the frozen protocol and limitations
listed here; it is not a universal claim.

## Core conclusion allowed by the frozen evidence

The defensible paper line is:

1. Q1: TerraState retains useful predictive skill on the frozen
   temporal-shift evaluation.
2. Q2: its state-mediated path is load-bearing under clean removal; the
   identity-transition diagnostic is supportive but confounded.
3. Q3: replacing future weather changes endpoint behavior and degrades
   fidelity on the frozen matched stress test.

Q4/composition, extreme-specific enhancement, causal counterfactual validity,
strict public ranking, and SOTA are not established.
