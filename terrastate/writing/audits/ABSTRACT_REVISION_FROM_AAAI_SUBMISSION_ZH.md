# TerraState 摘要修订：基于 AAAI 原提交版本

## 修订后的英文摘要

High-resolution satellite time series are a primary tool for monitoring vegetation, agriculture, and ecosystem response, and are increasingly cast as weather-driven forecasting: predicting future land-surface observations from cloud-obscured image histories and meteorological drivers. Yet such models are selected almost entirely by fixed-horizon pixel accuracy, which cannot establish whether their internal representations act as reusable world states rather than one-shot predictors. An accurate forecaster may still ignore the weather forcing, collapse toward persistence, or expose a latent state that does not actually carry the forecast—failures that standard error metrics cannot detect. We introduce **TerraState**, a testable predictive-state world model that augments a forecasting backbone with a spatial predictive state inferred from cloud-masked histories and a shared transition conditioned on future weather, geography, and elapsed time, requiring this state to carry a measurable contribution to the forecast. Rather than asserting a world state by architecture, TerraState makes this claim falsifiable through state-contribution removal, a supporting identity-transition control, and matched interventions that compare actual future weather with matched-donor and normalized-mean weather. On GreenEarthNet under temporal distribution shift, TerraState retains useful forecasting skill; removing its state contribution degrades performance on both validation and OOD-t splits, with paired confidence intervals excluding zero, while, on a frozen heat–drought subset, actual weather yields more faithful endpoint predictions than matched-donor and normalized-mean controls, supporting a load-bearing and weather-responsive predictive state.

## 完整中文对应版

高分辨率卫星时间序列是监测植被、农业和生态系统响应的主要工具，并且日益被建模为天气驱动的预测任务：根据受云遮挡的历史影像和气象驱动因素预测未来地表观测。然而，这类模型几乎完全依据固定预测时域的像素精度进行选择，而这一标准无法判定其内部表示究竟发挥着可复用世界状态的作用，还是仅服务于一次性预测。一个准确的预测器仍可能忽略天气强迫、退化为接近持续性预测，或暴露出一个实际上并未承载预测的潜在状态——这些失效无法由标准误差指标检测。我们提出 **TerraState**，一个可检验的预测状态世界模型：它在预测骨干中引入由云掩膜历史推断的空间预测状态，以及由未来天气、地理信息和经过时间共同条件化的共享转移，并要求该状态为最终预测承载可测量的贡献。TerraState 不凭借网络架构直接断言模型具有世界状态，而是通过移除状态贡献、作为支持性证据的恒等转移对照，以及比较真实未来天气、季节与地理匹配的供体天气和归一化平均天气的匹配干预，使这一主张能够被证伪。在 GreenEarthNet 的时间分布偏移设置下，TerraState 保留了有用的预测能力；移除其状态贡献会使 validation 和 OOD-t 划分上的性能均下降，且配对置信区间均排除零；同时，在冻结的热旱子集上，真实天气相较匹配供体天气和归一化平均天气产生了更忠实的端点预测，从而支持 TerraState 学得了一个真正承载预测且响应天气条件的预测状态。

## 修改边界

- 本次只删除了已经放弃或尚未得到冻结证据支持的 Q4/composition、旧天气对照和 matched-backbone 主张。
- 未加入 SOTA、严格优越性、因果天气响应、热旱特异增强或可复用读出等超出 Q1–Q3 的结论。
- 实验发现只出现在最后一句；前五句依次承担背景、问题、失效模式、方法定义和可证伪机制。
